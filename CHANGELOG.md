# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Separate "Text size" and "Window size" settings (Options tab).
- The workout card grows to fit long instructions instead of cutting them off.
- Trial of Strength: the number box no longer covers "last time".
- Long quest names in the card header end in "..." instead of overlapping.
- A quick double-click on "Done" no longer finishes two sets, or answers "Too easy" by accident.
- Trial of Strength: a number much bigger than last time (a typo like 120 for 12) asks you to confirm.
- An automatic Trial no longer grabs the keyboard, so you can still move and chat.
- Playing past midnight: the first set after midnight starts the new day's count.
- Several level-ups in one dungeon give one workout; the rest are saved for later.
- Dying several times in a row gives at most one death set per quest-snack gap.
- A workout put off with "Later" stays away if you pause for the day.
- Being AFK resets the stand-up reminder.
- No more "No player named ... is currently playing" lines from stats sharing.
- Two characters with the same first name keep separate records.
- Long setting names wrap instead of running under the buttons; the minimap notice stays on screen.

## 0.2.0 (first public release)

- Quest turn-ins give a short exercise snack, level-ups a mini workout, and every 10 levels a
  Trial of Strength that updates your numbers.
- Five starting tiers, from Peon (very out of shape, nothing on the floor) to Hero (very fit).
- Every exercise has a looping picture showing how to do it.
- Your numbers adjust to you: answer "Too easy", "Just right" or "Too hard" after each workout.
  Growth is limited to 10% a week. Exercises move up or down a ladder (wall, counter, knee, full push-ups).
- Gentle start: the first two weeks begin at 70% and build up, and again after 10+ days off.
- Swap button: a kinder exercise for the same muscles when something hurts.
- Quiet mode (no jumping), pause for today, stretch breaks, eight gentle stretches.
- Daily limit in sets and a weekly goal in training days. Once you reach them, reminders only
  suggest standing up or stretching.
- Real-life fitness level 1-60 with titles, rested XP for days off, progress shared across all
  characters on your account.
- Campfire nudges and stand-up reminders: small notices by the minimap, never popups.
- Never pops up in combat, waits until you leave dungeons and raids, and keeps at least
  5 minutes between workouts.
- Optional stats sharing with other Azeroth Fit users (tooltip line, Buddies list).
  No leaderboards. /flex shows your stats only to you unless you choose a channel.
- Text and window size setting.
